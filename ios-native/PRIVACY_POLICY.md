# Privacy Policy for Plan Your Perfect Day

**Last Updated:** January 24, 2026

## Introduction
"Plan Your Perfect Day" ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our iOS application.

## Data We Collection
- **Location Data**: We ask for your location to provide city-specific recommendations and routing. This data is processed locally on your device or sent ephemerally to our servers for the sole purpose of itinerary generation. We do not track your location history.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience.

## Data Storage
- **Local Storage**: Your itineraries and preferences are stored locally on your device.
- **Backend Processing**: When you request an AI-generated itinerary, your query is sent to our secure servers (Firebase Functions + Gemini API). We do not store these queries permanently associated with your identity.

## Third-Party Services
- **Google Places**: Used for venue information.
- **Apple Maps**: Used for routing and display.

## Your Rights
You can revoke location permissions at any time via iOS Settings. You can delete your locally stored data by deleting the app.

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@londondayplanner.com
