# App Store Submission Guide

## 1. App Store Connect Setup

### App Information
- **Name**: Plan Your Perfect Day
- **Subtitle**: AI Travel & Day Planner
- **Category**: Travel / Lifestyle
- **Content Rating**: 4+

### Keywords
london planner, day trip, travel ai, itinerary maker, city guide, nyc travel, liquid glass

## 2. Required Assets

### App Icon
- 1024x1024px PNG (no alpha)
- Place in `Assets.xcassets/AppIcon`

### Screenshots (iPhone 6.9" Display)
Resolution: 1320 x 2868 pixels

1. **Splash Screen**: "Beautiful Liquid Interface"
2. **Home View**: "Ask for your perfect day" (Input query: "Romantic date in Soho")
3. **Itinerary View**: "AI-Curated Timeline" (Show venue cards)
4. **Map View**: "Interactive City Maps" (Show pins)
5. **Share Sheet**: "Export to PDF & Calendar"

## 3. Build & Archive

1. Set Active Scheme to "Any iOS Device (arm64)".
2. Product -> Archive.
3. Validate App in Organizer.
4. Distribute App -> TestFlight & App Store.

## 4. Privacy Details (App Privacy Label)

- **Location**: Precise Location (Product Integration)
- **User Content**: Customer Support (if applicable)
- **Identifiers**: Device ID (Analytics, optional)

## 5. Review Notes

- Demo Account not required (no login wall).
- "Liquid Glass" UI is a stylistic choice designed for iOS 26.
